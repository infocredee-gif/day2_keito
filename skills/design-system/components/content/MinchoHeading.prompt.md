Mincho headline — the brand's editorial voice. Sans-serif is for UI, mincho is for statements.

```jsx
<MinchoHeading level={1} overImage>もみ殻からケイ素を抽出、<br />世界で初めて溶液化しました。</MinchoHeading>
<MinchoHeading level={2}>特許製法により純度98%以上。</MinchoHeading>
```

Level 1: 50px, navy #14315c, heavy display mincho, shadowed when over the hero photo. Level 2: 36px slate #4c5f70 with the 3px orange left rule. Keep line breaks manual — the site breaks Japanese lines by meaning.
